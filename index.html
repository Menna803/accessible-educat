About the project

Creating an Inclusive Higher Education Environment

Our project focuses on improving accessibility and inclusion for students with disabilities in higher education.

🎓

Our Mission

To raise awareness about the challenges students with disabilities face in universities and encourage educational environments where every student can participate equally.

🤝

Our Vision

A university environment where buildings, learning materials, technology, and student activities are accessible to everyone regardless of their abilities.

The Problem We Are Addressing

Students with disabilities may face physical, academic, technological, and social barriers in higher education. Inaccessible buildings, unsuitable learning materials, limited assistive technology, and lack of awareness can affect their participation and academic success. Accessibility should be considered from the beginning of university planning rather than treated as an afterthought.

Why Does This Matter?

Higher education provides knowledge, skills, independence, and opportunities for future employment. When students cannot access classrooms, resources, or activities, they do not receive the same opportunities as other students. Inclusive education benefits the entire university community by encouraging diversity, respect, cooperation, and equal participation.

Understanding the problem

Barriers Students May Face

Accessibility challenges can appear in different parts of university life. Recognizing them is the first step toward change.

01
🏢

Physical Barriers

Stairs without ramps, inaccessible elevators, narrow entrances, and classrooms that are difficult to reach can limit movement around campus.

02
📚

Academic Barriers

Learning materials that are not available in accessible formats, inflexible teaching methods, and unsuitable examination arrangements can affect learning.

03
💻

Technology Barriers

Websites, online platforms, and digital documents that do not support assistive technologies can make learning resources difficult to use.

04
🗣️

Communication Barriers

Lack of captions, sign language support, clear instructions, or alternative communication methods may prevent full participation.

05
👥

Social Barriers

Stereotypes, negative attitudes, isolation, and limited awareness can affect students' confidence and sense of belonging.

06
🏛️

Institutional Barriers

Limited support services, unclear procedures, and insufficient accessibility policies can make it difficult for students to receive the help they need.

Research and evidence

What Previous Research Tells Us

Research highlights the importance of listening to students and identifying the barriers that affect their university experiences.

Hong (2015)

Research examining barriers experienced by college students with disabilities in higher education. It highlights the importance of understanding students' experiences and the challenges they face.

Majoko (2018)

Research focusing on the participation and voices of students with disabilities, emphasizing the importance of listening to their experiences when developing inclusive education.

Li et al. (2024)

Research discussing academic learning experiences and challenges faced by students with disabilities in postsecondary education.

Gull, Kaur & Basha (2026)

A systematic review discussing challenges faced by students with disabilities in higher education and the need for better accessibility and support.

Research-Based Recommendations

Universities should improve physical accessibility, provide learning materials in different formats, adopt accessible digital platforms, train staff, increase awareness, and involve students with disabilities in decisions that affect their education.

Practical Solutions for Inclusive Universities

Accessibility requires cooperation between universities, educators, students, and the wider community.

♿

Accessible Buildings

Provide ramps, elevators, accessible restrooms, clear signs, and classrooms that students can reach independently.

📖

Inclusive Teaching

Offer flexible teaching methods, accessible documents, alternative formats, and reasonable examination arrangements.

⌨️

Assistive Technology

Support screen readers, captions, keyboard navigation, speech-to-text, and other tools that help students access information.

👩‍🏫

Staff Training

Train university staff and educators to understand accessibility needs and provide appropriate support.

💬

Student Participation

Include students with disabilities in university decisions, feedback sessions, and the design of support services.

🌍

Awareness Campaigns

Encourage respect, challenge stereotypes, and help the university community understand the importance of inclusion.
